Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, role (individual, engineer/surveyor, or admin), and password (stored securely, hashed — GeoNEXA never sees your plain-text password).
  • Usage data: parcels and locations you search or save, AI Assistant conversations you initiate, and reports you generate.
  • Payment records: plan, amount, billing cycle, and payment status. Card numbers and mobile money PINs are handled entirely by our payment processors (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Flutterwave) — GeoNEXA never stores them.
  • Location search terms: when you search a place by name, that search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find coordinates. GeoNEXA does not currently access your device's GPS location.

2. How we use it

  • To provide core features: parcel lookup, mapping, AI analysis, reports, and the engineer marketplace/community chat.
  • To manage your subscription and process payments.
  • To improve the platform and respond to support requests.

3. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google Gemini — powers the AI Assistant. Messages you send to the AI Assistant are processed by Google's API to generate a response.
  • MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Flutterwave — payment processing.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — location search on the map.

We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data retention & your rights

We keep your account data as long as your account is active. You can request account deletion or a copy of your data by contacting us below. Deleting your account removes your profile and saved data; payment records may be retained briefly for accounting/legal purposes.

5. Children's privacy

GeoNEXA is not directed at children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

6. Changes to this policy

As GeoNEXA adds features, this policy may be updated to reflect what data is collected — for example, if we later introduce analytics tools or device-location features, we'll update this page before rolling them out.

7. Contact

Questions or data requests: geosageairw@gmail.com
