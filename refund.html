Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Our approach

GeoNEXA handles refund requests on a case-by-case basis. We don't guarantee automatic refunds, but if something went wrong on our end — a payment charged twice, a subscription that never activated, or a feature that didn't work as described — reach out and we'll review it fairly.

How to request one

  • Email geosageairw@gmail.com with your account email, the plan you paid for, and a brief description of the issue.
  • Include your payment reference or the date/time you paid, if you have it — this helps us find the transaction faster.
  • We aim to respond within a few business days.

What to expect

If a refund is approved, it will be returned via the same payment method you used (mobile money or card), where technically possible. Processing time depends on the provider and is outside GeoNEXA's control.
